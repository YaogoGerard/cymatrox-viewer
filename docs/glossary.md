# Glossary

- **Cymatics** — the study of visible standing-wave patterns produced by sound.
- **CymaScope** — an instrument that images a cymatic figure on a liquid
  surface. The Fluid module imitates its top-down view.
- **Cymaglyph** — a single CymaScope image, i.e. one Faraday figure.
- **Chladni figure** — the pattern sand forms on a vibrating plate, collecting
  along the nodal lines. This is the Granular module.
- **Faraday wave** — a standing wave on a vertically driven fluid surface,
  appearing at half the drive frequency. This is the Fluid module.
- **Nodal line** — a curve of zero displacement in a standing wave; where the
  sand piles up.
- **Mode `(m, n)`** — a standing-wave solution labelled by its azimuthal order
  `m` (the number of petals) and radial order; for a circular dish the radial
  part is a Bessel function.
- **Granular** — a material of discrete grains (sand). Here, the particle-based
  simulation of a Chladni plate.
- **Latest-wins** — a communication policy where only the newest value matters
  and intermediate ones may be dropped (the `SimParamsSlot`).
